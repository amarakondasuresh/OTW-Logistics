# Admin Reports – Layout Fit

- Desktop sidebar reduced from 245px to 224px (200px up to 1100px wide). Phone and small tablet: the sidebar is
  off-canvas and takes no width from the page.
- Content padding reduced; the content area takes the remaining width (min-width:0, max-width:100%).
- Reports summary cards: seven cards on one even responsive grid (no short second row), amounts in ₹ Indian format.
- Filters: even responsive grid; Apply Filter and Reset Filter sit on one aligned row.
- Tables: columns fit the card. Only the wide rider table scrolls horizontally, and only on screens narrower than 1480px.
- Colours, fonts, branding and navigation are unchanged. All overrides are at the end of dashboard/styles.css.
